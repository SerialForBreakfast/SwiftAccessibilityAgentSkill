# Accessibility Nutrition Labels: Support Claims

Source review: 2026-11-04. Tier-1: App Store Connect overview and management guidance.

Evaluate support against common tasks on each supported device, rather than a single
accessible screen or the presence of an API. Include advertising and app extensions
when they participate in those tasks. For third-party content, provide discoverable
ways for creators to supply accessible content. Match each label to its feature criteria.

The overview describes label display on OS 26+ across Apple's platforms. Feature
applicability varies by device. This display boundary is separate from the OS where
an individual app feature, such as tvOS Larger Text, becomes available.

Publish and update device-specific answers in App Store Connect. Compatible iPhone/
iPad apps on Apple Vision Pro or Apple-silicon Macs use their iPhone/iPad responses
as described by Apple; native platform apps require their applicable device answers.

These criteria govern an app's support claims. They do not require manual device
evidence before this skill accepts Apple documentation as platform guidance.
