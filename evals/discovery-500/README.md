# Discovery 500: find decisions the accessibility skill can change

**Prepared, not dispatched. No grades yet.** This is a failure-enriched development set, not 500 confirmed failures or a transfer benchmark. The goal is an accessibility decision that was wrong unaided and correct with the skill under matched conditions.

| Questions | Why they are here |
|---:|---|
| 300 | Six different deliverables for each of 50 previously failed questions: design decision, incident triage, integrated solution, discriminating proof, preservation review and acceptance plan. Prior whole-task failures include evidence and unnecessary rework, not solely accessibility defects. |
| 150 | 80 code reviews and 70 proposed patch reviews from pinned fixtures. Sixteen reviews present working implementations; patch directions include both fixes and regressions. |
| 50 | Product briefs combining decisions, alternating between design and acceptance. Cross-platform analogues motivate questions but do not establish API behavior. |

iOS: 151 UIKit and 62 SwiftUI. macOS: 83 AppKit and 69 SwiftUI. tvOS: 78 UIKit and 57 SwiftUI. Report per-question rescues **and** distinct developer decisions; related questions share evidence. Questions reuse known development material. The fixture platform distribution follows the existing evidence, not population prevalence.

[Qualification of all 19 previous cases](QUALIFICATION.md) records the alternate implementation, rejectable behavior and remaining boundary. It does not certify the 500 as natively graded. [Grading guide](GRADING.md) separates behavioral decisions, ordinary actions, integration, submitted builds and requested proof. Only subject Markdown is model-visible. [Held BigDog handoff](HANDOFF.md) describes the single return, scope and cost boundary. Subject ZIP, controller and grading hashes are in [the freeze](freeze.json).

Offline checks (from this directory): run `python3 -B qualify19.py`, `python3 -B build.py --check`, and `python3 -B test_packet.py`. They verify historical receipts and generated bytes, not model performance or delivered accessibility. Treat disputed, underspecified or non-discriminating items as grading gaps rather than failures. Before opening any candidate answers, freeze unaided grades, the exact skill revision, and the selection of failures and preserved controls for matching.
