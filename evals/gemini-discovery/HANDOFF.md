Required fields: id, arm (unaided/challenger), sealSHA256, inputSHA256 (common question), skillSHA256 (null or sealed archive hash), model, cohort (same pinned model revision/settings/transport, excluding arm), verdict and primary (pass/fail/inconclusive/not-run), critical (boolean). Source and native evidence must remain distinguishable in the located grade. Complete and partial reports must list unattempted cells separately. Omitted rows never become passes.

A fully rescued decision requires an observed unaided primary failure, whole-answer passes on all affected paired failures, and comparable outcomes without regression throughout that decision's registered cases. Supplementary cases without registered decision keys still reveal regressions and affect the release decision. For reporting uncertainty, group by decision family; repeats and cross-platform analogues are not independent samples. A critical newly lost action blocks promotion irrespective of totals.
