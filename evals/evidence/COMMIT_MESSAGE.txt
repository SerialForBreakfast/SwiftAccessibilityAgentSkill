Prepare evidence-backed accessibility skill update and evaluation baseline

Establish a measurable update path for the proposed 0.2.3 candidate while
keeping the released skill identity at 0.2.2 until evaluation gates pass.

Baseline and evaluation
- Capture 217 pre-update working-tree files with SHA-256 fingerprints.
- Preserve the original snapshot and knowledge-only overlay as separate
  comparison stages rather than rewriting historical evaluation inputs.
- Add a shared model client, dependency-free runner and promptfoo adapter
  for generic, instruction-only, injected-reference and traced-lookup cases.
- Add 60 candidate scenario families: 40 development and 20 held-out, with
  OS contexts, multi-turn conversations, negative cases and evidence gaps.
