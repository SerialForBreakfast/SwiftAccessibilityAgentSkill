## Corrections to Earlier Reasoning

- A failing rerun is not proof an API changed. SDK, runtime, test setup, oracle and
  harness may differ. Record the discrepancy before assigning its cause.
- A green suite is not complete coverage. The rotor initial-search gap was found
  after an earlier suite passed; it is not a recorded pre-fix crash.
- Intentional mutations are test-sensitivity evidence, not spontaneous model errors.
- The historical model revision and full context were not recorded. These observations
  cannot establish that other models are worse or that the skill prevents the failures.
- Explicitly configuring a button trait does not settle what assistive technology
  exposes for a native button. Avoid converting a headless observation into a universal rule.
- Saying work will be drafted is not evidence it was written. Completion reports
  must name artifacts and checks, with pending reviews/runs stated separately.
- Earlier task examples mention manual/UI verification. For this scoped work the
  later explicit unit-only decisions govern; old records remain historical.

## What Worked

effective-environment assertions, characteristic-based media selection, and meaningful
negative controls made defects inspectable. Source/test maps and frozen hashes made
evidence auditable. These are candidate practices to evaluate, not proven skill effects.
