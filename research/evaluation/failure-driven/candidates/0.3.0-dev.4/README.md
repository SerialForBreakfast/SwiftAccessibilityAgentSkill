owner fails closed. Missing sufficiently specific source mappings stay explicit;
there is no substitution of a merely similar guideline. Candidate overlays are

## Evaluation cadence

Develop repeatedly without frontier calls. Consolidate material changes into one
milestone. Require local integrity/compile-unit evidence, alternative-aware reviewed
questions, a changelog-to-test map and full-context size accounting before considering
a frontier batch. A version/commit does not trigger evaluation. The earlier 14-call
dev.3 proposal remains historical and unused, not an active routine allocation.

No live model/provider wrapper is included here. Future allocation must be explicitly
approved with exact provider/quota, cells, complete payload hashes and token/time
ceilings. Use one representative suite spanning changed behaviors and preservation/
no-change controls; preserve original responses and compile/unit-check them locally
before considering more generation. Repetitions target material uncertainty, not
every wording edit. Historical answers are diagnostic unless the comparison identities
are sufficiently matched. Future generic/released/candidate conditions are needed
for overall skill-lift claims; known incidents are development, not new holdouts.
