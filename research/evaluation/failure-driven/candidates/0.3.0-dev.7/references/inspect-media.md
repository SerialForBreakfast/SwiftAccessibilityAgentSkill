Inspection is read-only. Distinguish no item, loading, read failure, successful absence and positive metadata. Associate both success and error with the item and current request. External player-item replacement needs an explicit observation or reload trigger; cancellation alone cannot disqualify a late result. Compare the item's actual selected options in audible and legible groups before and after inspection; local IDs, volume and mute are not selection evidence. A metadata flag describes a track, not the quality of its description or completeness of captions.

When editing a selection rather than inspecting, bind Off as well as concrete options to the displayed item/snapshot. Preserve a selected option outside the filtered list as current/unlisted. Only offer clearing if the group permits it. Do not replace the player. Use the companion selection example only if the caller requests implementation detail.
