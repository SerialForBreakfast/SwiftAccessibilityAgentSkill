Choose an assertion for the claim: a compile check establishes the spelling for a specific SDK and deployment target; a direct unit can establish the configuration, state or action it observes on its recorded runtime. Neither establishes delivered focus, rendered layout, generated semantics or speech. Keep source, diagnostics and environment from the first failure; distinguish a subject error from a wrong expectation or broken setup. A deliberate mutation must reach the intended assertion. Describe unexecuted checks as proposals and keep manual and UI tests outside a compile/unit-only request.
