First decide whether explicit focus restoration is needed. Closing a presentation and completing its dismissal are different events. When restoration is required, tie it to the owning presentation's actual completion or equivalent lifecycle signal. Do not treat a close request or a binding becoming false as proof of completion. Cancellation must not restore as though the presentation closed. Consume a matching request before invoking reentrant code; late or duplicate callbacks must leave a newer presentation intact.

Recheck membership and eligibility in the originating owner. Use the requested fallback if the original target is no longer usable. In AppKit, a visible enabled view can still refuse first responder. In SwiftUI and tvOS, a binding update or preferred focus request is not evidence that focus arrived. Preserve correct native behavior rather than posting an additional notification on every dismissal.
