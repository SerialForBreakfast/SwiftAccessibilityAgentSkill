exemplars: correct, defective, valid alternative and ungradable for each scenario.

or completed calibration. Do not fill them with stock boilerplate and claim coverage.

the criterion; record exact input/output bytes, original grades, independent grades
and disagreement resolution. Tests or helper names alone do not settle whole-answer
quality. A deterministic transport fixture does not calibrate an accessibility judge.

No model judge or exemplar-generation batch is authorized. Calibration can be
prepared/reviewed in the ordinary authorized maintenance workflow; any separate
provider evaluation still needs explicit allocation. Qualify the transport first
so this larger authoring effort does not optimize for an unavailable experiment.
