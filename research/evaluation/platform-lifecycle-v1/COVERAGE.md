[initial inventory](../platform-expansion-v1/COVERAGE.md) and
[navigation delta](../platform-navigation-v1/COVERAGE.md). Parity means comparable
source-backed behavioral families, not equal method counts or copying iOS APIs.

New claims map to macOS m-007/m-009 and tvOS t-002/t-008/t-009. Each is a bounded
subset, not completion of every guideline clause. SDK27 compilation is distinct
from Mac27 or TV26.5 runtime observations. Existing source tiers remain intact.

## Next consolidated batch

1. Audit AVFoundation/AVKit selection, loaded-item metadata and missing-data
   contracts against each platform's declarations. Identify equivalent behavior
   and genuinely platform-specific controls before implementation.
2. Build Mac and TV positive/negative evidence together; pair each new behavioral
   family with an independent natural question and accepted-alternative rubric.
3. Extend coverage into native forms/state recovery and Mac outline/column
   relationships after the media audit. Keep runtime/request limits explicit.
4. Review the13 currently drafted platform questions as separate versioned sets;
   only the original nine belong to the prepared BigDog/Gemini plan.

Independent review assesses the oracles and questions. It does not require the
owner to manually validate accessibility interactions. Existing skill and baseline
promotion remain benchmark-backed decisions after evaluation.
