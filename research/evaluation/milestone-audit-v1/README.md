## Forward-use harness repairs

[guards.mjs](guards.mjs) wraps the original modules without rewriting them:

- `safePreflight` rejects malformed nested cases/plans, paths, hashes, budgets and
  review flags with structured errors. The original preflight throws for null plans.
- `guardedCompare` rejects explicit unavailable identity/accounting descriptions,
  including the preserved phrase `not independently exposed in response`. The old
  arithmetic accepted that phrase as a matching model revision in a synthetic
  example. Original numeric eligibility was not authorization to promote or run.

The wrapper does not authenticate model origin or hidden weights. Genuine versioned
IDs still pass through unchanged. These guards are available for a future revised
package; they are not silently wired into frozen historical reports. Synthetic
counterexamples have complete inputs and observed original/guarded results. They
are controller tests, not frontier or accessibility failures.
