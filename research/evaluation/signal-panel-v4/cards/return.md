A close request, cancellation and completed dismissal are different events. Keep the origin independently of the presented content. Complete only the owning dismissal, consume its return request before calling reentrant code, and resolve the destination against what is eligible then.

For a sheet, take the ordinary Close path before completing it. Contrast an origin that survives with one that disappears. Bind the callback to that presentation or serialize presentations. Passing a known ticket to a helper does not exercise an installed callback that reads the current ticket.

For overlapping UIKit panels, restore or transfer the original background snapshot before the next panel captures it. Finish the replacement too: suppression while it is open says nothing about the final state. Preserve an initially hidden background and the panel's ordinary controls.

For AppKit, check ownership by the originating window. An absent origin and an attached one that declines first-responder eligibility can both require fallback; removing a button only checks the first. Distinguish eligibility from the result of an attempted request. Apply the specified fallback policy; report the target requested, not delivered assistive focus.
