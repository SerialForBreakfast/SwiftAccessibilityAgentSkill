Inspection is read-only. Distinguish missing item, checked empty group, failure and actual selection. An active option excluded from the menu is not Off. Identify described audio via .describesVideoForAccessibility, not language or title; identify positive and ordinary fixture tracks independently of that predicate.

For a choice, load the current item's asset group, validate membership, call item.select(option, in: group), then publish currentMediaSelection.selectedMediaOption(in: group). Off requires allowsEmptySelection; automatic is different. Bind the picker setter, including nil, to its originating item and request: a replacement can use the same item. A stale or foreign choice must leave playback unchanged.

With a real local group and a different initial selection, drive the installed binding. Check actual caption and unrelated audio selections; compare old and current Off after reload.
