## What survived cross-checking

The first submission is the better summary of the experiment and its limits. Use neither narrative as the case registry. The sealed grades and traces support nine whole-task gains, no whole-task losses, four preserved passes and forty persistent failures. Three pairs are incomplete. Nine gains span eight families; only four of those nine also changed primary correctness. The single source grader did not execute the returned code. The matched dismissal flags are serious even though neither is a new whole-task loss. The invoice flag lacks an unaided answer.

The second submission's exposure split reproduces: 12/35 whole passes after a corroborated body read versus 1/19 without one. All nine rescues had reads. This is not a randomized comparison of readers: neither failure to read nor a specific sentence can be assigned causal responsibility. Its platform annotations are not reliable (e.g. P-d76c is tvOS UIKit); use the joined table. The seven persistent answers with media-card reads do not all have the same defect: wrong fixture selection, absent item, wrong symbol, and missing player writes require different checks. No installed validation-card reads were corroborated.

Four startup failures and an interrupted reservation are infrastructure dispositions. The continuation script labels its replacement grant owner-approved; a script label is not independent evidence of authorization. Preserve the run and its limitation; do not treat remaining quota as permission to resume after an isolation stop.
