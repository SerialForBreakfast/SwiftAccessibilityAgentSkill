Identify the framework that owns the interactive child. In AppKit, consider the parent's role together with its exposed children; a composite row does not by itself justify hiding independently editable or actionable children. In a SwiftUI wrapper, check which native target the updated view actually invokes. In a reusable UIKit view, follow the installed button or action, not just a helper on the model.

For an action retained across refresh, distinguish the identity it captured at installation from the owner at invocation. A detached control and a live control reparented to another owner are different cases. Preserve the still-current ordinary action as well as rejecting a stale one. Avoid adding a duplicate activation or sending a focus notification solely to update a label.
