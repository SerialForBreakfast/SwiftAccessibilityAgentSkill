# Paired scoring guide v1 (controller only)

Review the complete developer request and original answer. Use the same scoring.json row in both arms. Each criterion supplies the positive standard, counterexample, treatment of missing evidence, permitted alternatives and bounded source/assertion evidence. Clarifications qualify the inherited rule. Grade behavior, not keywords or similarity to an example. None of the 24 tasks is a fresh holdout or newly qualified code gold.

1. Mask identity/routing preambles and condition labels; retain the original and a hash of each masked answer. Randomize answer order with a recorded controller-only seed before a separate grader sees them. Disclose residual clues, prior exposure and imperfect masking. Do not present references or answer IDs that reveal arm assignment to the grader.
2. For each criterion record meets, material gap, or not assessable, with a span of the answer and the reason. Do not turn a missing native observation into a source-level defect. Record integration separately: an isolated helper may be correct while its actual wiring is missing. Missing answers, truncation or transport errors are ungradable, never semantic failures. A whole-task judgment needs the application's full requested path, not an average of criteria.
3. For comparability record correctness/task success/evidence calibration/relevance on 0-4 (absent, materially faulty, partial, substantially correct, complete); weights .40/.35/.15/.10, divided by four. Do not award answer-quality points for branding or ROUTING formatting. Score truthful evidence attribution where requested; record first-turn identity and routing-format compliance separately.
4. Compare each pair: gain, regression, persistent gap, preserved success, or inconclusive if either side cannot be judged. Report denominators per framework/platform and family; do not count 48 sessions as 48 independent problems. A newly lost ordinary action or broken navigation blocks release regardless of the average. Report paired differences and limitations; these known development cases and this transport do not establish general lift.
