# ADR-005: Start the skill guidance from observed failures

**Decision:** adopted for a research challenger only. Haiku 5.5 low is the development target; evaluate on other frontier coding models after value is shown. No release, remote execution or spend is authorized. The released skill and rc.1 remain separate, unchanged comparators.

## Context and drivers

We found compact-candidate experiments, but not a completed clean-start experiment with rule-removal comparisons. [Compact A](../evaluation/failure-driven/compact-candidate-a/PLAN.md) combined edits. [Dev.5 vs generic](../evaluation/gemini-controlled-run-20261107/README.md) showed 13 vs 9 meets on 24 paired tasks, not universal improvement. [Dev.7](../evaluation/dev7-flash-haiku-results-20261107/README.md) used fewer tokens on Flash but gained one case and regressed on three. [rc.1](../../evals/rc1/package/build.py) assembled inherited notes. In the [explicit replay](../../evals/rc1-failure-replay/RESULTS.md) all seven sessions loaded rc.1; checkout integration and media ownership still failed after relevant reads. The five passes are not five attributed fixes.

We want substantial batches, ordinary controls that remain usable, and claims that distinguish real execution from plausible explanations. The [paired font contracts](../../evals/headroom/checkout-font/README.md) show why compression needs counterexamples: automatic tracking can satisfy a live label's contract but defeat a caller-supplied size. Neither a general ban nor a general prescription follows.

## Decision and invariants

Start the instructional text at zero. Reuse reviewed Apple sources, versioned unit examples, hidden oracles, alternatives, negatives and packaging conventions. Build the same installable skill name into isolated research archives, never into a second discoverable tree. Hold metadata, first-response identity and actual-read attribution constant across challenger variants. Neither a missing announcement nor an unused reference is itself a failed code contract.

Review all active development prompts together. Admit a behavioral rule only with a recorded graded failure, a decision-changing counterexample and a neighboring preservation case. Label evidence by model, platform, grader and scope. A failure on Flash is an observation on Flash, not one on Haiku. A deliberate mutant or new pool fixture is not an observed model failure. Do not attribute causation to pooled historical observations.

Maintain a short shared entry point; put decisions specific to a task/framework in focused cards. Keep questions, original answers, solution variants, assessment criteria, evaluation history and rule-removal manifests controller-only. For each iteration, try deleting, merging, narrowing or moving each rule. Preserve a distinction if removing it changes the correct recommendation. A newly compiled example validates an example, not a prompt intervention.

## Validation and decision rights

Compare shell/full explicitly on identical inputs to estimate the package effect. Compare frozen rc.1/full under the same conditions for the incumbent decision. Compare full versus full-minus-one-family on a target, counterexample and neighboring case to study marginal value *given the other rules*. Interacting rules may require a separately proposed joint removal. Evidence from a bundle-wide win cannot establish which sentence helped.

Freeze input bytes, grading contracts, accepted alternatives, first-attempt policy, repeat selection and arms before execution. Use native compilation/units for observable behavior, masked review for residual claims, and separate reporting for discovery vs invoked quality. Use predeclared family-level paired uncertainty, preserve incomplete pairs, and show all critical regressions regardless of average. Distinguish bytes from measured tokens. Reserve untouched transfer families for after freeze; different wording of a known incident is not an untouched family. Report inconclusive comparisons as such.

You set quota, scope and interruption policy. The local project prepares one packet; BigDog receives one authorized job and returns one self-contained evidence bundle. No live job is implicit in packaging. No promotion without evidence of transfer, review of platform regressions and satisfaction of the applicable release gates.

## Alternatives and consequences

- Incrementing rc.1 retains breadth but cannot isolate the value of inherited guidance; keep it as the incumbent instead.
- One instruction per prompt rewards memorization; one execution per edit spends quota without measuring neighboring regressions. Prefer parallel family authoring followed by a paired portfolio comparison.
- A full automatic optimizer introduces model calls and search choices before we have a reliable task distribution. Borrow [CheckList's contrastive behavior tests](https://aclanthology.org/2020.acl-main.442/), [ProTeGi's batch critiques](https://arxiv.org/abs/2305.03495) and [GEPA's trace-driven reflection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457), not their budgets or reported gains.
- A failure-only skill can be too narrow. Publish unknown cells, preserve known successes and route new risks into discovery. Minimal means the shortest *tested* variant preserving measured outcomes, not globally optimal instructions.
