Apple platform evidence. They do not establish API contracts or universal bug ranges.

## Extraction checklist

For every user-impact report record author/editorial status, publish/update date,
OS build, device, assistive technology/settings, exact task, symptom, workaround,
claimed fixed version and corroboration. Preserve differences between observations.

Prioritize braille, VoiceOver rotor familiarity, ordinary navigation, Switch Control,
Voice Control, keyboard-only interaction, low vision, hearing access and cognitive
load. Ask whether shortcuts supplement rather than replace discoverable actions.
Separate novice setup/discovery barriers from experienced-user efficiency concerns.
