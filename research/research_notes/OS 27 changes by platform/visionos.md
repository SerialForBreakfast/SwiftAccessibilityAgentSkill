## Q1. Entry-by-entry inventory of the visionOS 27 release notes: section, status, Apple issue number, SDK-linked conditions, workaround

### Takeaway
The three visionOS 27-family release-note documents hold **168 bullets** (VISION-101 to VISION-268): 27.0 has 160 bullets across 53 sections, 27.2 has 6, and the 27.3 beta has 2. No visionOS 27.1 release-notes document exists, because no visionOS 27.1 release shipped (see Q5). None of the release-note bullets describes a user-facing accessibility feature. Look to Drive, Dwell Control, Vehicle Motion Cues and Face Gestures are documented only in support.apple.com material. Accessibility-adjacent developer items are `TabsPickerStyle`, which VoiceOver reads as "tabs"; `LabeledContent` in `Menu`, which maps to a subtitle; the RealityKit `.highlight` hover-effect fix; a known eye-tracking regression from Eye Setup; and look-to-talk Siri activation.

## Q2. Which May 2026 accessibility features shipped in visionOS 27.x, in which point release, and with what hardware, region or safety restrictions?

### Takeaway
Of the visionOS items announced on 2026-05-19, Apple's own update notes confirm only **Look to Drive** (the wheelchair control) and the **updated Dwell Control** (Glance Up to Tap) as shipped in **visionOS 27.0** (2026-09-14). **FaceTime third-party sign-language interpreter / live captioner** support is confirmed for **visionOS 27.2** (2026-10-26). **Vehicle Motion Cues** and **Face Gestures** have live visionOS 27 User Guide pages, so they are shipped, but they are not itemized in the update notes; their point release is inferred as 27.0. **Generated subtitles** and **improved MFi hearing-aid pairing** have announcement evidence only. Look to Drive carries the heaviest restrictions: TOLT or LUCI controllers only, U.S. and U.S. territories only, an adult trusted supporter, controlled environments, a battery above 10%, and no use on wall power.

## Q3. Which developer APIs exist for spatial accessibility in 27, and what is their DocC availability?
